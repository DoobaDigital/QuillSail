What happens

Posts keep their dates, tags and images. Drafts stay drafts. The first category becomes the section.

Liquid and plugins are never run. Common tags are converted, and anything else is listed so you can fix it.

Importing sends no email and doesn't notify anyone. Running it again skips posts already imported.

Old addresses redirect only if this site answers on your old domain. GitHub Pages can't send redirects, so a blog moving off github.io needs jekyll-redirect-from on the old site.
